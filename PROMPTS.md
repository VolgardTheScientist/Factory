# AI Image Generation Prompts for Virtual Gallery Sub-Rooms

These prompts are engineered to generate the sub-room background images required for the Virtual Gallery. They are designed to precisely match the moody, decaying industrial aesthetic, shadow depth, and spatial perspective of the provided Main Hall reference image.

## General Style Guidelines (Apply to all prompts)
- **Aesthetic:** Decaying industrial, brutalist architecture, moody, atmospheric, cinematic lighting.
- **Lighting:** Dimly lit, harsh directional light, deep volumetric shadows, glowing digital screens providing high-contrast localized illumination.
- **Textures:** Raw concrete, rusted metal, peeling paint, exposed pipes and wiring, dust motes in the air.
- **Perspective:** First-person wide-angle view, spatial depth, leading lines drawing the eye inward.
- **Color Palette:** Desaturated cool tones (grays, blues) contrasted with warm, neon glows (orange, cyan, magenta) from digital elements.
- **Negative Prompt:** Bright sunlight, clean surfaces, modern office, cheerful, flat lighting, low contrast, text, watermarks.
